How to talk about it

Interview Prep

Structure your past experience using the STAR method to clearly demonstrate your strategic capabilities during interviews.

Situation

"At my previous role, we were losing market share to a new competitor because our feature set was outdated."

Task

"I needed to redefine our product strategy for Q3 to regain user trust without halting ongoing development."

Action

"I analyzed competitor gaps, aligned engineering and marketing on three core strategic pillars, and phased the rollout."

Result

"We stabilized churn within two months and saw a 15% uptick in feature adoption, proving the new strategic direction."
